## Limits

- Part A's spellings were chosen by us; real resumes may contain others.
- Short abbreviations with no lexical overlap (e.g. `k8s`) are the hardest case for embeddings.
- Similar skills are suggestions for the recruiter, not proof of equivalent experience. Two different programming languages can score as similar (e.g. `Python~Java`), which is the weakest pairing.
- Similarity weighting raises scores for candidates with related skills but rarely changes who ranks first.
- The generated test resumes use clean skill names, so Part B does not exercise the embedding pass.
- No labelled hiring outcomes were available, so this measures extraction quality, not hiring accuracy.
